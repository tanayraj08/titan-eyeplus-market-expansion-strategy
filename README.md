# Titan Eye+ — Scaling to Compete in India's Eyewear Market

A strategy case study examining Titan Eye+'s scale gap versus Lenskart, India's largely unorganized eyewear market, market prioritization, and a dual-channel growth strategy.

## Strategic Question

**How can Titan Eye+ scale meaningfully in India's eyewear market while preserving its trust-led positioning and profitability?**

## Executive Summary

Titan Eye+ has a profitable operating base but a much smaller revenue and store footprint than Lenskart. The case frames the growth opportunity around converting demand in a market where approximately 80% remains unorganized, using a **dual-channel model**:

- **Trust-led offline expansion** across Tier-2 / Tier-3 markets
- **Digital acceleration** in large, online-ready urban markets
- **Selective premium presence** where brand visibility and economics justify it

The case uses a directional **Organized Conversion Opportunity Score (OCOS)** to structure market prioritization.

## Key Findings

| Metric | Case figure |
|---|---:|
| India eyewear market, 2024 | **$6.10B** |
| Unorganized share | **~80%** |
| Unorganized-market opportunity | **~$4.88B** |
| Titan Eye+ FY25 revenue | **₹796Cr** |
| Titan Eye+ FY25 EBIT margin | **10.7%** |
| Lenskart FY25 revenue | **~₹6,652Cr** |
| Lenskart India store footprint | **2,137** |
| Titan Eye+ store footprint | **~900** |
| Analyst revenue scenario | **₹1,600Cr by FY29E** |

> The $4.88B figure is calculated as 80% of the 2024 market and is described as an **unorganized-market opportunity**, not a strict SAM.

## Case Structure

### 1. Diagnose the Scale Gap

Titan Eye+ operates from a profitable base, but its FY25 revenue of ₹796Cr is substantially below Lenskart's ~₹6,652Cr. The public figures also show a meaningful difference in store footprint.

[Scale Gap Exhibit](visuals/scale_gap.png)

### 2. Understand Titan's Portfolio Context

Eyecare contributes approximately 1.4% of Titan Company's FY25 revenue, versus the much larger Jewellery business.

[Titan Revenue Mix](visuals/titan_revenue_mix.png)

### 3. Size the Market Opportunity

India's eyewear market is presented at $6.10B in 2024, up from $3.17B in 2014, with a 2028E market size of $8.15B.

[Market Growth](visuals/market_growth.png)

Approximately 80% of the 2024 market is described as unorganized. Applying that share to the $6.10B market produces an analyst-calculated ~$4.88B unorganized-market opportunity.

[Market Opportunity](visuals/market_opportunity.png)

### 4. Prioritize Markets

The original **OCOS** framework combines:

1. Brand-Trust Sensitivity
2. E-Commerce Readiness
3. Competitive White Space

The scores are directional and illustrative rather than an established industry metric.

[Market Prioritization](visuals/market_prioritization.png)

### 5. Execute Through Dual Channels

**Foundation — Months 1–6**
- Establish city-level priorities
- Strengthen digital acquisition and CRM
- Test Tier-2 / Tier-3 expansion formats

**Acceleration — Months 7–18**
- Expand winning offline formats
- Increase digital reach and conversion
- Integrate online-to-offline customer journeys

**Defend & Compound — Months 19–48**
- Scale proven city clusters
- Improve retention and customer lifetime value
- Use network density and brand visibility to compound growth

### 6. Financial Scenario

The case presents an **analyst-created scenario** of:

**₹796Cr FY25 → ₹1,150Cr FY27E → ₹1,600Cr FY29E**

with a 10%+ EBIT-margin assumption.

[Growth Scenario](visuals/growth_scenario.png)

This is **not Titan management guidance**.

## Methodology & Limitations

This case combines figures presented in the source case material with analyst calculations and original frameworks.

### Source-derived
- India eyewear market figures
- Titan Eye+ FY25 revenue and EBIT margin
- Titan Company FY25 revenue mix
- Lenskart FY25 revenue, store count and cited organized-market share

### Analyst-calculated
- 2014–2024 market CAGR
- Organized / unorganized split from the stated 80% unorganized share
- ~$4.88B unorganized-market opportunity
- Revenue scale multiple

### Original analysis
- OCOS framework
- Directional market prioritization
- FY27E / FY29E revenue scenario

### Important comparability note

Titan Eye+'s **10.7% EBIT margin** and Lenskart's cited **4.5% PAT margin** are different profitability measures and should not be interpreted as a like-for-like margin comparison.
